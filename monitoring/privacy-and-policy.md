1. Introduction
Welcome to ft_pong This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled within the scope of this academic project developed at 42SP. Our goal is to ensure data transparency and security, following the core principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data We Collect
To provide a functional gaming and social experience, we collect:

Identification Data: Username and unique student ID (retrieved via 42 Intra OAuth).

Game Data: Match history, scores, wins, losses, and performance statistics.

3. Purpose of Data Collection
Your data is used exclusively for:

Managing user authentication and profiles.

Powering the matchmaking, tournament, and leaderboard systems.

4. Data Sharing
All data collected in this project is stored locally within Docker containers. We do not share your data with third parties or use it for commercial purposes.

5. Right to be Forgotten (Data Deletion)
In compliance with the GDPR, you have the right to request the total deletion of your data.

By selecting the "Delete Account" option in your profile settings, all your personal data, match history, and statistics will be permanently removed from our database.

6. Security
We implement technical security measures, such as protected environment variables (.env). These measures are designed to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
